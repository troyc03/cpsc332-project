# Candidate official data sources

These are authoritative source portals/indicator pages to investigate for real observations. The repository's current 2023 indicator rows are synthetic and must not be attributed to these sources. Record the exact retrieved value, year, units, download/API URL, and retrieval date for any value used.

- World Bank GDP per capita (current US$), indicator NY.GDP.PCAP.CD: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD
- World Bank unemployment (total, modeled ILO estimate), SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS
- World Bank poverty headcount ratio at $2.15/day (2017 PPP), SI.POV.DDAY: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SI.POV.DDAY
- World Bank current health expenditure (% GDP), SH.XPD.CHEX.GD.ZS: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.XPD.CHEX.GD.ZS
- World Bank physicians per 1,000 people, SH.MED.PHYS.ZS: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.MED.PHYS.ZS
- World Bank maternal mortality ratio, SH.STA.MMRT: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.STA.MMRT
- World Bank literacy rate, adult total (%), SE.ADT.LITR.ZS: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.ADT.LITR.ZS
- World Bank government expenditure on education (% GDP), SE.XPD.TOTL.GD.ZS: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.XPD.TOTL.GD.ZS
- World Bank gross tertiary enrollment, SE.TER.ENRR: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SE.TER.ENRR

Some indicators may not have 2023 values for every country. Use the latest available year only if the team explicitly documents the year per country; do not silently fill missing data or invent observations. Also confirm the poverty definition/year and indicator definition before comparing.
